4. String voltage check (cold)

Panel open-circuit voltage (Voc) rises as it gets colder. Use the coefficient from your panel's datasheet; −0.28%/°C is typical. Keep at least 5% margin below the controller's maximum.

5. Charge controller capacity

Usable panel watts ≈ amps × ~14.3 V per 12 V of battery. For example, a 70 A controller handles about 1,000 W at 12 V. More panel watts than that is fine; the controller just clips them at midday.

Estimates only, not engineering advice. Measure real loads, follow the manuals for your equipment, and use proper fusing. Formulas: array W = Wh/day ÷ (PSH × derate); battery Ah = Wh/day × days ÷ (usable fraction × 0.95) ÷ battery V.
